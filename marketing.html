// Good to Know

Questions,
Answered

Quick answers about SEO, paid ads, email, reporting and pricing.

What digital marketing services does LUNAX Marketing offer?

+

LUNAX Marketing covers 12 disciplines: SEO, PPC advertising, social media marketing, content marketing, email marketing, affiliate and influencer marketing, conversion rate optimisation, analytics, e-commerce marketing, mobile marketing and video marketing. We plan them as one full-funnel strategy, from the first impression to the final sale.

Which advertising platforms do you manage?

+

We run paid campaigns on Google Ads (Search, Display, Shopping and Video), Meta (Facebook and Instagram), LinkedIn, TikTok and Amazon, including retargeting. For marketplace ads on Walmart, eBay, Etsy and bol.com, see our e-commerce management.

How long does SEO take to show results?

+

SEO usually takes several months, often three to six, to show meaningful ranking and traffic gains, depending on your competition and your website's starting point. Technical fixes and quick wins can show sooner, and results keep compounding over time.

Should I invest in SEO or paid ads?

+

Paid ads bring traffic immediately and are ideal for launches and testing offers, while SEO builds organic traffic that keeps working without paying for every click. Most businesses do best with both: ads for fast results while SEO grows. We recommend the right mix after an audit.

Do you offer local SEO and Google Business Profile optimisation?

+

Yes. Local SEO is part of our SEO service, including Google Business Profile optimisation, local keywords and location pages, so you show up when nearby customers search for what you offer.

Which email marketing platforms do you work with?

+

We specialise in Klaviyo and also integrate email with your CRM. We set up automated flows such as welcome, abandoned cart and post-purchase sequences, segment your list, and track the revenue each email generates.

How do you measure and report results?

+

We set up Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager and attribution tracking, then build custom dashboards so you can see what each channel delivers. We report on the KPIs agreed in the strategy, such as leads, sales, ROAS and customer acquisition cost.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

+

Yes. Our team is based in Lahore, Pakistan, and runs campaigns for brands worldwide. Strategy calls, reporting and campaign management all happen online.

How much do digital marketing services cost?

+

Pricing depends on which channels you need, how competitive your market is and the scale of your campaigns. Your advertising budget is usually separate from the management fee. You get a clear proposal after a 30-minute call.

How do I get started?

+

. We start with an audit of your current channels and performance, then recommend the strategy and channels that will move your numbers first.
