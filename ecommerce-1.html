// How It Works

The Process

01
🧭

Discovery

We evaluate your current store, analyze competitors, review product positioning, and identify gaps in your sales funnel. From platform setup to conversion flow, we build a clear ecommerce strategy designed around your revenue goals.

02
🏗️

Build

We set up or rebuild your Shopify store and marketplace accounts with optimized product pages, structured collections, secure payment integrations, and essential apps. Every element is built for speed, performance, and a seamless customer experience across mobile and desktop.

03
🚀

Launch

Before going live, your store is fully tested and optimized. We implement tracking systems like Meta Pixel and Google Analytics, refine checkout flow, configure shipping and tax settings, and ensure everything is ready to drive sales from day one.

04
📈

Scale

After launch, we focus on growth. Through conversion rate optimization, A/B testing, email automation, upsell strategies, retargeting campaigns, and marketplace expansion, we turn traffic into consistent revenue and long term brand growth.

// Good to Know

Questions,
Answered

Quick answers about platforms, advertising, fulfilment, results and pricing.

What e-commerce management services does LUNAX Marketing offer?

+

LUNAX Marketing provides end-to-end e-commerce management: store setup and Shopify development, listing and SEO optimization, product image design, marketplace advertising such as Amazon PPC, catalog and inventory management, conversion rate optimization and performance reporting. Our team in Lahore, Pakistan, manages stores for brands selling in the US, Europe and worldwide.

Which marketplaces and platforms do you manage?

+

We manage Shopify, Amazon, eBay, Walmart Marketplace, Etsy, TikTok Shop, ManoMano, Cdiscount and bol.com. Each platform gets its own strategy for listings, advertising, pricing and operations, so you can sell across several channels with one partner.

Do you manage Amazon PPC and marketplace advertising?

+

Yes. We run Amazon PPC and Sponsored Ads, Walmart Sponsored Products, Etsy Ads, TikTok Shop Ads, eBay Promoted Listings, and sponsored campaigns on bol.com and Cdiscount, optimizing bids, keywords and budgets against your sales and profit targets. For Google and Meta Ads, see our digital marketing services.

Can you set up Amazon FBA and Walmart Fulfillment Services?

+

Yes. We handle FBA setup and fulfilment strategy for Amazon and WFS integration for Walmart, together with inventory planning, so your stock is in the right place and your products qualify for faster delivery options.

Can you help us expand into European marketplaces?

+

Yes. We launch and manage stores on bol.com for the Netherlands and Belgium, Cdiscount for France, and ManoMano for home and DIY buyers across Europe, with localized listings, catalog feeds, pricing and sponsored ads for each market.

Can you build a new Shopify store from scratch?

+

Yes. We design and develop Shopify and Shopify Plus stores with optimized product pages, structured collections, secure payments and essential apps, then set up tracking, shipping and tax before launch. See our website development services for fully custom builds.

How do you measure and report results?

+

We set up tracking such as Meta Pixel and Google Analytics, monitor each marketplace's performance dashboards, and report regularly on sales, conversion rate, advertising spend and return, account health and inventory.

How long does it take to see results?

+

Advertising and listing optimization usually show measurable changes within the first few weeks, while organic rankings, reviews and multi-channel expansion build over several months. We agree on clear KPIs at the start and track them together.

How much does e-commerce management cost?

+

Pricing depends on the number of platforms, the size of your catalog and whether you need setup, ongoing management, advertising or all three. You get a clear proposal after a 30-minute strategy call.

How do I get started?

+

, share your store links and goals, and we'll review your current setup and recommend where to start.
