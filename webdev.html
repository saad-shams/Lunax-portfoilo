// Good to Know

Questions,
Answered

Quick answers about platforms, redesigns, hosting, timelines and pricing.

What website development services does LUNAX Marketing offer?

+

LUNAX Marketing builds business websites, Shopify stores, WordPress and WooCommerce sites, high-converting landing pages and full-stack web apps with React, Next.js and Node.js. Every build is responsive, fast and SEO-ready. Our team is based in Lahore, Pakistan, and works with clients worldwide.

Do you build Shopify stores?

+

Yes. We build and customise Shopify stores, including custom themes and sections in Liquid, app integrations and headless storefronts. After launch, we can also run and grow the store through our e-commerce management services.

Should I choose Shopify, WordPress or a custom Next.js website?

+

Choose Shopify if you mainly sell products online, WordPress if you want an easy-to-edit content site or blog (with WooCommerce for smaller stores), and a custom React or Next.js build if you need top performance, complex features or a web app. We recommend the right option after a short discovery call.

Will my website be fast and SEO-friendly?

+

Yes. We build with Core Web Vitals in mind, using optimised images and fonts, server-side rendering where it helps, clean headings, meta tags and structured data, so search engines and AI assistants can understand your site.

How long does it take to build a website?

+

It depends on the scope. A landing page is much quicker than a full online store or a custom web app. After the discovery call, we agree on a clear timeline with milestones before development starts.

How much does a website cost?

+

The cost depends on the platform, the number of pages and the features you need. A landing page, a Shopify store and a custom web app are each priced differently. You get a clear quote after a 30-minute call.

Do you provide support after launch?

+

Yes. After launch, we monitor errors and analytics, and we can keep improving your site with updates, new features, A/B tests and performance tuning.

Can you redesign my existing website?

+

Yes. We rebuild slow or outdated websites and migrate sites between platforms, for example from Wix or WordPress to Shopify or Next.js. We keep your important URLs and set up redirects to protect your existing Google rankings.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

+

Yes. Shopify and WordPress sites come with easy editors for pages, products and blog posts, and custom builds can include a content management system, so your team can update content without a developer.

Do you handle hosting, domain and launch?

+

Yes. We set up your domain, SSL and hosting, and deploy on platforms such as Vercel, Cloudflare and AWS, or on Shopify for online stores. Analytics and error monitoring are in place from launch day.

How do I get started?

+

, tell us about your business and what the website needs to do, and we'll recommend the right platform, scope and next steps.
