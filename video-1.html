Questions,
Answered

Quick answers about our video editing services, formats, pricing and process.

What video editing services does LUNAX Marketing offer?

+

LUNAX Marketing edits short-form and long-form videos for creators and brands: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikToks, anime, manga, movie and TV recap videos, AI and tech explainers, and vlogs. Every edit can include motion graphics, captions, music and colour grading.

Which platforms do you edit videos for?

+

We edit for YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook. Each video is exported in the right format for its platform: vertical 9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, and widescreen 16:9 for YouTube.

What software do you use, and what quality do you deliver?

+

We colour grade in DaVinci Resolve and deliver in 4K or 8K at 24 or 60 frames per second, depending on your footage and where the video will be published.

How long does a video edit take?

+

It depends on the length and complexity of the video. Short-form edits are usually turned around faster than long-form recaps or vlogs. We confirm a delivery date before work starts, so you can plan your upload schedule.

How much does video editing cost?

+

Pricing depends on video length, complexity and how many videos you need. Single edits are priced per video, and creators who publish regularly can choose a monthly plan. You get a clear quote after a 30-minute call.

Do you edit videos for clients outside Pakistan?

+

Yes. Our editors are based in Lahore, Pakistan, and work with creators and brands worldwide. You share your footage through a cloud link such as Google Drive or Dropbox, and we deliver the finished files online.

How do I get started?

+

, share a sample of your footage and the style you like, and we'll recommend the best format and editing approach for your channel or brand.
