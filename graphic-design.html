Questions,
Answered

Quick answers about our graphic design services, deliverables, timelines and pricing.

What graphic design services does LUNAX Marketing offer?

+

LUNAX Marketing designs brand identities and logos, social media creatives, email campaigns, UI/UX for websites and apps, Amazon and Shopify product listing images, infographics, packaging and advertising creatives. Our design team is based in Lahore, Pakistan, and works with brands worldwide.

What is included in a brand identity package?

+

A full brand identity covers brand strategy, a logo and icon mark, colour palette, typography, a moodboard, stationery and brand guidelines that show how to apply everything consistently. You can see complete examples in our Frappe Froth, Rawayat and Brix projects above.

Do you design Amazon and Shopify product listing images?

+

Yes. We design product listing images for Amazon, Shopify and other marketplaces, including feature highlights, infographics and lifestyle compositions that communicate your product's value instantly. They pair well with our e-commerce management services.

How many design concepts and revisions do I get?

+

For logos and brand identities, we present three unique design concepts. You choose the direction that fits your brand best, and we refine it based on your feedback before final delivery.

What files will I receive?

+

You receive editable source files plus print-ready and web-ready exports, such as PDF, SVG and PNG for logos, and correctly sized files for social media, email and online marketplaces.

How long does a graphic design project take?

+

It depends on the scope. Single assets such as social media posts or listing images are quicker than a full brand identity, which includes research, concepts and refinement. We agree on a timeline before work begins.

How much does graphic design cost?

+

Pricing depends on the type and number of designs you need. A single logo, a set of social media templates and a full brand identity are each priced differently. You get a clear quote after a 30-minute call.

How do I get started?

+

, share your brand, goals and a few designs you like, and we'll recommend the right package and next steps.
