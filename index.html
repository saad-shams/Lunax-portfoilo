What does LUNAX Marketing do?

+

LUNAX Marketing is an AI-driven e-commerce and digital marketing agency based in Lahore, Pakistan. We help brands grow online through e-commerce store management, paid advertising, social media management, website development, graphic design and video editing, with every strategy guided by data and measured against real business results.

Which e-commerce platforms do you work with?

+

We manage and grow stores on Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy and bol.com. That covers listing optimization, product visuals, PPC campaigns, Shopify development and full store management, so you can expand into new marketplaces without building an in-house team. See our e-commerce services.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

+

Yes. Our team is based in Johar Town, Lahore, and we work with brands worldwide. Calls, reporting and project updates all happen online, so your location and time zone are never a barrier.

How does LUNAX use AI in its marketing?

+

We use AI as a tool, not a gimmick: to speed up market and keyword research, analyse campaign and sales data, test creative and copy variations, and automate repetitive workflows. Our team reviews every output, so decisions stay strategic and grounded in your numbers.

How long does it take to see results?

+

It depends on the channel. Paid ads and listing optimization usually start producing measurable data within the first few weeks, while organic growth from SEO and social media builds over several months. We agree on clear KPIs at the start and report on them regularly.

How much do your services cost?

+

Pricing depends on your goals, the services you need and the size of your store or ad spend. After a 30-minute strategy call, we send a clear proposal covering scope, deliverables and cost, so you know exactly what's included before you commit.

Do you take on one-off projects or only monthly retainers?

+

Both. We take on one-off projects such as a new website, brand identity or a single video edit, and we also provide ongoing monthly management for online stores, ad campaigns and social media.
